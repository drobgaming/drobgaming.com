Privacy Policy — drobgaming

Short version: this app collects nothing. Last updated November 18, 2026.

What this app collects

Nothing. The drobgaming app collects no personal data itself. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is recorded, stored, or sent anywhere by the app.

The want-list form

The want-list form in Shop the Vault does not send or save anything itself. When you tap “Text it” or “Email it,” the form just opens your own text or email app with your message already filled in. Nothing is transmitted until you press send in your own app — and when you do, the message goes through your own carrier or email provider, the same as any other text or email you send.

Calling, texting, and emailing David

If you choose to call, text, or email, David receives what you send — your number or email address and your message — so he can reply and help with your order. That contact happens through your phone and email services, not through this app, and the app keeps no copy of it.

Links to other sites

Some buttons open other sites and apps — eBay, Whatnot, TikTok Shop, YouTube, and Instagram. Anything you do there, including buying, is covered by that service's own privacy policy, not this one.

Changes & questions

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here and in the app. Questions about privacy? Ask David directly: info@drobgaming.com · +1 (917) 409-8632.
